# Labs Overview

This section provides an overview of the **8 Comprehensive Hands-On Labs** included in the **Nutanix & Multi-Cloud Administration Lab Guide**.

## 🧪 Lab 01 — Building & Deployment of VMs in vCenter and Nutanix

Hands-on administration of **virtual machine (VM) building and deployment** using:

* VMware vCenter
* Nutanix

## 🛠️ Lab 02 — Managing Physical/Virtual Servers — Patching with WSUS & SCCM

Hands-on management of **physical and virtual servers**, including patching activities using:

* WSUS
* SCCM

## 💻 Lab 03 — Managing VMware ESXi 5.5 / 6.5 / 7.0 & Host Upgrades

Hands-on administration of VMware ESXi environments across:

* VMware ESXi 5.5
* VMware ESXi 6.5
* VMware ESXi 7.0
* ESXi Host Upgrades

## 💾 Lab 04 — Disk Expansion, Datastores, Snapshots, Migration & Nutanix Alerts

Hands-on administration covering:

* Disk Expansion
* Datastores
* Snapshots
* Migration
* Nutanix Alerts

## 🌐 Lab 05 — VM Clones, Templates & Networking (VLANs, DNS, DHCP, Routing)

Hands-on VM and network administration covering:

* VM Clones
* VM Templates
* VLANs
* DNS
* DHCP
* Routing

## 🔍 Lab 06 — Health Checks for VMs and ESXi Hosts

Hands-on health monitoring and validation for:

* Virtual Machines (VMs)
* VMware ESXi Hosts

## ⚙️ Lab 07 — DRS, HA & vMotion Management

Hands-on management of VMware virtualization capabilities, including:

* DRS — Distributed Resource Scheduler
* HA — High Availability
* vMotion

## 🖥️ Lab 08 — VDA Reinstall, VLAN Changes & SCCM Package Deployment

Hands-on administration covering:

* VDA Reinstallation
* VLAN Changes
* SCCM Package Deployment
